# weeks/

One file per week: what we recommended, what was actually started, and how it
turned out. The point is the **counterfactual** — not just "did I win", but
"would the other choice have scored more".

## Why bother

Every recommendation here is a judgment call made under uncertainty with
projections that are noisy. A single week tells you almost nothing; twenty weeks
of honest records tell you whether the advice is actually beating the
set-it-and-forget-it baseline. Recording only the wins makes the log worthless.

**Log the misses in the same detail as the hits.** A recommendation that looked
right and lost is different from one that was wrong on the reasoning, and only
the written record distinguishes them later.

## Convention

* `week-NN.md`, zero-padded (`week-01.md`).
* Fill the **Before** section when setting the lineup; fill **After** once the
  week's games finish.
* Record the reasoning at decision time, not reconstructed afterward — the
  reasoning is the part worth grading.
* Note when a recommendation was *not* followed, and why. That's a data point.

Start from `TEMPLATE.md`.
