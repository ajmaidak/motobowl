# Season review after week 11: lineups, draft, or luck?

My weekly totals from these pages match every weekly log (134, 119, 107, 133,
107, 110, 132, 83, 96, 96, 92).

## Answer

**Mostly the draft, then luck. Lineups have been average.**

* **Draft: last by a wide margin.** Players I drafted produced 72 started
  points a week. The league median is 105, and the next-worst team got 90.
  That is about −33 a week.
* **In-season adds: 2nd best.** Players I picked up (waivers, plus players
  other teams drafted and dropped) produced 38 started points a week. The
  median is 20, and only Pipefitters (39) got more. That recovered about half
  the draft gap.
* **Lineups: average.** 89.4% of the best possible lineup's points (6th of
  10, median 89.7%), with 13.1 a week left on the bench (3rd fewest).
* **Luck: worst in the league, −1.7 wins.** The all-play record (24–75, .242)
  predicts 2.7 wins, not 1. Four losses were by 2, 1, 3 and 11. Neutral luck
  makes this about 3–8, which is still last.

## Caveats

* "Optimal" uses each week's final roster on the matchup page. It ignores
  locks (a player added after his game counts), so it overstates what was
  achievable for every team equally.
* The Yahoo projection column uses the projections shown on the matchup page
  after the week, which may not be the projection at lock.
* Defenses were matched to draft picks by NFL team, since DEF picks have no
  player link on the draft page.
